# Helmeted card artwork

`helmeted-player.webp` is original decorative artwork created with the built-in image generation tool, then optimized to a small transparent WebP. It is anonymous, not a photograph or likeness of any roster player. Player names, numbers, ratings and traits are rendered separately from authoritative app profiles.

Prompt: One isolated front-facing upper-body American football athlete, entire helmet and broad shoulder pads visible, lower-chest crop. Photorealistic premium sports-game rendering. Graphite black helmet, silver facemask, opaque smoked visor, blank charcoal jersey without number, text, logos or team identity. Chrome white rim lighting with subtle plum reflections, detailed fabric and metallic helmet highlights, neutral ready pose, transparent background. No card frame, background, text or watermark. Anonymous decorative game artwork, not a portrait of any real player.
