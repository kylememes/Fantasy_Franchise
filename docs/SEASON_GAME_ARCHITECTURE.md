# Weekly seasons and authoritative football

The inaugural season uses the checked-in 2026 NFL preseason (Weeks 1–3, excluding the separate Hall of Fame exhibition) and regular-season schedule. Schedule facts are imported from official NFL weekly pages and validated for unique weekly participation, 17 regular-season games and one bye per franchise. Real scores are never imported.

Draft completion exposes a staging dashboard at Preseason Week 1. The commissioner explicitly opens the week. CPU-only games enter a durable queue immediately. An owner starts a CPU matchup by joining and readying; two human owners must both ready for their matchup. Readying may be cancelled before kickoff. Kickoff atomically freezes effective depth charts, player profiles, saved philosophies, staff names, and a private deterministic seed.

A pure, versioned TypeScript football engine produces a complete event timeline and statistics outside the browser. A Supabase Edge worker claims leased database jobs, computes the engine output, and commits only with its current lease. Expired leases are retryable using the original inputs and seed. Private job/results tables have no client access. A scheduled database dispatcher uses short-lived, single-use worker tickets, so no credentials are placed in source or sent to browsers. Authenticated league members can also wake their league's worker.

CPU games finalize immediately. Human games reveal saved events at a server-owned play cursor; elapsed server time drives live progression. Authorized participants can pause, resume, change speed, step to the next play, or simulate to the end. Polling cannot reroll results. Disconnects preserve the cursor. Completed games expose the immutable timeline and box score; each viewer independently controls replay speed and position. The renderer interpolates the actual engine's routes, rushes, ball flight, pursuit and ending location. It never resolves a play.

Week controls lock the league and check the expected phase/week. Advance requires every current game to be final. Simulate Unfinished queues unstarted games and finalizes prepared live games, without silently advancing. Repeated or racing operations cannot duplicate games, jobs, scores or advancement. Week 3 transitions to Regular Season Week 1; Week 18 ends this regular-season release. Playoff/offseason systems are a subsequent phase, not invented results.

Standings and player statistics are derived from immutable game results, with preseason separate. Staff hires are cosmetic real-name selections with one employment per coach per league and one person per staff slot; there are no invented coach ratings or automatic changes to owner tendencies. Empty CPU staff slots use a generic CPU staff. Owner settings remain editable for future games, but cannot alter a game's frozen kickoff inputs.

Verification covers schedule invariants, deterministic replay, 22 unique players, saved starter usage, coaching and matchup effects, ensemble statistical ranges, clock/field/scoring invariants, authorization, two-owner readiness, single-use tickets, job leases, private results, final immutability, and racing commissioner advancement.
